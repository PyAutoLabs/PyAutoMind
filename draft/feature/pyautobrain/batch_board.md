**Re-scoped by the human 2026-08-31 (see the epic's "Development through use"
section): not a second published surface.** The batch state is a strip on the
Mind dashboard, placed **above the "Start here" section** — the first thing the
human sees when they open the dashboard. The original standalone-board scope is
recorded in the epic and closed; do not rebuild it.

## What the strip renders

A single compact "Batches" block, generated by the same collect the dashboard
already runs (`_intake.py` — the markdown section builder near the "## Start
here" emit at `agents/conductors/intake/_intake.py:2027` and its HTML twin at
`:2325`; the strip is emitted immediately before both):

- **Latest packet** — link to the newest `batches/packets/<date>-<slot>.html`
  (the Pages URL `https://pyautolabs.github.io/PyAutoMind/packets/…`), with its
  `generated:` stamp.
- **Review state** — whether `batches/reviews/<date>-<slot>.md` exists for that
  packet: `review submitted` or `awaiting your review`.
- **In-flight batch** — if the newest batch record has no `collected:` yet: its
  member count, `review-at:`, and a PENDING note.
- **Archive** — one link to `batches/packets/` (the history).

Pure render over files that already exist; it decides nothing and recomputes
nothing. Every chip routes to the packet, the review file, or the batch record.

## Not in scope

Review actions (merge/tweak/reject live on the packet page and in the
orchestrator chat), gate-leg rendering, usage-window readings, any scheduled
pre-slot publish. If the strip ever needs its own collect, that is the
`batch collect` verb's job, not the dashboard's.

## Done when

- The dashboard (markdown and HTML) opens with the Batches strip above "Start
  here", correct with zero packets (strip says so), with a packet awaiting
  review, and with an in-flight batch.
- Regen is idempotent and the existing dashboard tests pass.
